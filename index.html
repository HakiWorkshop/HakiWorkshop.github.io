How this reference works

Buildings and troops: prices shown are base gold costs; costs can increase during a run. Open an entry to view every documented level of its upgrade paths. Walls and Gates share a sequential three-level research line.

Town Center: the keep begins with 10 HP and offers four upgrade branches. Choose up to two branches and upgrade each to level III. Barracks and Town Center improvements may affect other units across the field.

Balance note: this codex was reconstructed from EchoSwap V131 (November 14, 2026) and later design notes. Fire Thrower and Heavy Barracks are included, but their exact costs and upgrade trees require newer game code. Enemy tactical descriptions derive from the earlier enemy manual and may need a live-balance pass.
